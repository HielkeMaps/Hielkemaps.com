VeinMiner finds your ores and pickaxes by itself. There's no code to write: follow these naming rules and you're done.

1

Name your ores

Every block whose ID contains _ore is picked up as an ore, in any namespace except minecraft. These all work:

{% highlight text %}mygeology:ruby_ore mygeology:deepslate_ruby_ore mygeology:ruby_ore_nether{% endhighlight %}
2

Tag or name your pickaxes

Every item with the minecraft:is_pickaxe tag is picked up as a pickaxe. So is every item whose ID ends in _pickaxe, like mygeology:ruby_pickaxe, even without the tag.

3

Give your ores a loot table

VeinMiner drops exactly what your loot table drops, so Fortune and Silk Touch work the way you set them up. It also reads the loot table to measure which pickaxe tier an ore needs, so a stone pickaxe can't vein mine your diamond-tier ore.

4

Test it

Load a world with both Add-Ons. Open the VeinMiner book, then Settings › Add-Ons: your Add-On is listed with every ore and pickaxe VeinMiner found. Hold one of your pickaxes and look at an ore: the outline shows the whole vein.

Done!

Your ores and pickaxes now work with VeinMiner, with no extra setup for players.

Good to know

  • Blocks that differ only by deepslate, deep, nether or end are one ore. A vein mixing ruby_ore and deepslate_ruby_ore is mined in one go, and players see a single Ruby Ore in the settings.
  • Ores and pickaxes show the names from your Add-On's own .lang files, in the player's language. Your Add-On is listed under its namespace, so my_geology shows as My Geology.
  • The outline uses your ore's minecraft:map_color. Without one it's light grey.
  • Dropping items from a script instead of a loot table? VeinMiner's breaks don't fire your script, so when the loot table is empty it guesses the drop from the ore's name: mygeology:ruby, mygeology:raw_ruby or mygeology:ruby_ingot. A real loot table is more reliable.
  • Custom blocks can't drop XP orbs, so your ores give no XP when vein mined.
  • Detection is on by default. Operators can turn it off under Settings › Add-Ons, and players can switch single ores and pickaxes off.

Reference

How VeinMiner decides what your ores and pickaxes can do.

Mining tiers

A pickaxe vein mines an ore when its tier is at least the ore's tier. Both are measured in-game when the world loads:

TierOre needs at leastPickaxe gets this tier when it can mine
1Wooden pickaxeStone
2Stone pickaxeIron ore
3Iron pickaxeDiamond ore
4Diamond pickaxeObsidian
5Netherite pickaxeBuilt-in support only

An ore's tier is the lowest vanilla pickaxe that gets drops from its loot table. When that can't be measured, any pickaxe can mine it. A pickaxe that can't be measured counts as diamond tier.

Built-in support

Some Add-Ons have built-in support instead, like Tinker's Construct and More Ores & Tools. That's for Add-Ons that can't follow the rules above, for example ores that don't have _ore in their ID, or ores that should give XP. Does your Add-On need it? Get in touch.
