What is VeinMiner?

An Add-On for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that lets you mine an entire ore vein by breaking just a single block. It makes gathering resources and clearing big ore deposits a lot faster.

How do I configure VeinMiner?

Use the VeinMiner book. You get one automatically, and if you lose it you can craft a new one by combining a book with an iron ingot.

Does VeinMiner work with enchantments?

Yes! Fortune gives more drops and Unbreaking reduces tool damage. With Silk Touch, the ore blocks themselves drop instead of their resources.

Is VeinMiner compatible with other Add-Ons?

Yes, it works with many popular Add-Ons. See the compatible Add-Ons for the full list.
