What is VeinMiner?

An Add-On for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that lets you mine an entire ore vein by breaking a single block, up to 256 blocks at once. It makes gathering resources a lot faster.

How do I change the settings?

Use the VeinMiner book. You get one the first time you join a world, and if you lose it you can craft a new one from a book and a piece of coal.

How do I mine just one block?

Sneak while you mine. If you'd rather vein mine while sneaking, switch Activation to Sneaking in the settings.

Does VeinMiner work with enchantments?

Yes! Fortune, Silk Touch and XP work on every block, exactly as if you mined them one by one. Unbreaking saves durability.

How much durability does it cost?

One durability per extra block, like mining them one by one. If your pickaxe doesn't have enough, the highlight turns red and only the one block breaks. Creative mode costs nothing.

What is the VeinMiner Book?

It's for an optional challenge. When an operator turns on Required under Enchantment, only pickaxes with the VeinMiner enchantment vein mine. Find the books in structure chests or at the enchanting table, then tap an anvil with one to enchant your pickaxe.

Can every player have their own settings?

Yes. Activation, break animation and highlight are personal. Operators choose the world's defaults and can stop players from changing them.

Is VeinMiner compatible with other Add-Ons?

Yes. It finds other Add-Ons' ores and pickaxes by itself. See the compatible Add-Ons for the ones with built-in support.
