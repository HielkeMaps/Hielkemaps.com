Instant Vein Mining

One swing, and the whole vein is yours! Break a single block and every connected ore comes with it, up to 256 blocks at once.

Vein Highlight

See before you mine! Aim at an ore and the whole vein lights up. Pick from 7 patterns, in the ore's own colour or rainbow, with an optional pulse. It turns red when your pickaxe can't handle the vein.

All features

Break Animations

Watch the vein get pulled into your pickaxe, burst, spin or shrink away, or just break instantly.

Enchantment Ready

Fortune, Silk Touch and XP work on every block in the vein, exactly as if you mined them one by one. Unbreaking saves durability.

Customizable Activation

Vein mine while standing and sneak to mine a single block, or the other way around.

Made for Multiplayer

Every player picks their own activation, animation and highlight. Operators set the world's defaults and can lock them.

Pickaxe & Ore Settings

Choose which pickaxes vein mine, and which ores each pickaxe works on.

Pull Items to You

Have the drops and XP fly straight to you instead of landing where the ores were.

VeinMiner Enchantment

Want a challenge? Make vein mining need the VeinMiner enchantment, found in structure chests and at the enchanting table.

Ultimate Compatibility

Finds the ores and pickaxes of other Add-Ons by itself, on top of built-in support for popular ones.
