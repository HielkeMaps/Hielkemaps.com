  • Initial release of VeinMiner
  • Vein mining for every vanilla ore, including deepslate and Nether ores and ancient debris
  • Vein highlight with 7 patterns, ore-coloured or rainbow
  • 5 break animations: Absorb, Burst, Spin, Shrink and Instant
  • Fortune, Silk Touch and Unbreaking support
  • Per-player settings, with world defaults for operators
  • Optional VeinMiner enchantment, found in structure chests and at the enchanting table
  • Automatic support for other Add-Ons' ores and pickaxes
  • Built-in support for Tinker's Construct, More Ores & Tools, More Ores Tools Armor and Weapons+
