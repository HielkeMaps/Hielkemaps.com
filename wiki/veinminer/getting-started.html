1

Install the Add-On

  1. Get VeinMiner from the Minecraft Marketplace.
  2. Click Activate Add-On, then pick an existing world or create a new one.
  3. That's it!
2

Mine your first vein

  1. Hold any pickaxe in your main hand.
  2. Aim at an ore. The whole vein lights up.
  3. Break it, and the entire vein is mined at once!

VeinMiner works while you're standing. Sneak to mine just one block. You can swap the two in the settings.

Your pickaxe still matters. It needs the right tier for the ore, like in vanilla, and enough durability for the whole vein. If it doesn't have enough, the highlight turns red and only the one block breaks.

3

Open the settings

Use (right-click) the VeinMiner book and open Settings. Pick how VeinMiner activates, how the vein breaks and what the highlight looks like. These only change things for you.

Operators also get the world settings: which pickaxes and ores work, pulling items to players, Add-Ons and the VeinMiner enchantment.

You get the book the first time you join a world. Lost it? Craft a new one from a book and a piece of coal.

4

Using other Add-Ons?

VeinMiner finds the ores and pickaxes of other Add-Ons by itself, and has built-in support for some popular ones. Check the compatible Add-Ons to see what's supported.
