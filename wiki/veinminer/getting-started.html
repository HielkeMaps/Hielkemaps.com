1

Install the Add-On

  1. Get VeinMiner from the Minecraft Marketplace.
  2. Click Activate Add-On, then pick an existing world or create a new one.
  3. That's it!
2

Mine your first vein

  1. Hold any pickaxe in your main hand.
  2. Find an ore vein.
  3. Break any block in the vein.
  4. Watch the whole vein get mined at once!

By default VeinMiner activates while you're sneaking. You can switch it to activate while standing instead.

Tool tiers still count. You need the right pickaxe tier to mine each ore, just like in vanilla.

3

Open the settings

Use (right-click) the VeinMiner book to open the settings and tweak VeinMiner to your liking.

You get the book automatically. Lost it? Craft a new one by combining a book with an iron ingot:

4

Using other Add-Ons?

VeinMiner works with vanilla ores and with custom ores and pickaxes from many other Add-Ons. Check the compatible Add-Ons to see what's supported.
