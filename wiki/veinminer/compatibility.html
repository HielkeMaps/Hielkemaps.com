Pickaxes

Using an Add-On that isn't listed? It probably works anyway. VeinMiner finds ores and pickaxes from other Add-Ons by itself when the world loads. Operators can see what it found, or turn this off, under Settings › Add-Ons.

Something not working with your Add-On? Let me know by contacting me. Making an Add-On? See the integration guide.
