1

Install the Add-On

  1. Get TreeCapitator from the Minecraft Marketplace.
  2. Click Activate Add-On, then pick an existing world or create a new one.
  3. That's it!
2

Fell your first tree

  1. Hold any axe in your main hand.
  2. Break the bottom-most log of a tree.
  3. Watch the whole tree topple over!

By default TreeCapitator activates while you're standing. You can switch it to activate while sneaking instead.

3

Open the settings

Use (right-click) the TreeCapitator book to open the settings. Here you can pick the fall animation, turn on Auto Replant, set up the trunk outline and much more.

You get the book automatically. Lost it? Craft a new one by combining a book with any sapling:

4

Using other Add-Ons?

TreeCapitator works with vanilla trees and with custom trees and axes from many other Add-Ons. Check the compatible Add-Ons to see what's supported.
