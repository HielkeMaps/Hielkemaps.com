What is TreeCapitator?

An Add-On for Minecraft Bedrock Edition that lets you cut down an entire tree by breaking just the bottom log. It makes gathering wood and clearing forests a lot faster.

How do I configure TreeCapitator?

Use the TreeCapitator book. You get one automatically, and if you lose it you can craft a new one by combining a book with any sapling.

Does TreeCapitator work with all types of trees?

It works with all vanilla trees, Nether trees included, and with custom trees from many Add-Ons. In the Axe Settings you can choose which logs each axe works on.

Can I turn off the falling animation?

Yes. Open Tree Falling in the settings and pick Break Instantly. You can also choose Fall Straight Down or Disintegrate instead of falling over.

Does TreeCapitator replant trees?

It can. Turn on Auto Replant in the settings and a sapling is planted at the stump, taken from the tree's drops or your inventory. It's off by default.

Is TreeCapitator compatible with other Add-Ons?

Yes, it works with many popular Add-Ons. See the compatible Add-Ons for the full list.
