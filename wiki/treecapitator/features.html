New in 2.0

Falling Trees

TIMBER! Trees topple over in the direction you cut them, sink straight into the ground, or disintegrate block by block. Complete with creaking, crashing sounds and a camera shake when big trees land!

Auto Replant

Keep your forest growing! A sapling is planted at the stump automatically, taken from the tree's drops or your inventory.

All features

Instant Tree Felling

One chop, and the whole tree comes down. Say goodbye to tedious tree-chopping!

Visual Tree Outline

See exactly what you're about to fell. Pick a color (even rainbow!), add a pulse effect and set the thickness.

Smart Leaf Handling

Break leaves instantly or watch them decay fast. You can even choose what happens to Nether leaves.

Intelligent Tree Detection

Recognizes real trees and tells natural leaves apart from player-placed ones.

Customizable Activation

Stand tall or sneak low: you choose how to activate TreeCapitator.

Tool Durability

Take durability for every log (left) or just once for the whole tree (right).

Axe Customization

Want to restrict certain axes to certain logs? TreeCapitator has you covered.

Ultimate Compatibility

Works with vanilla trees and axes, plus custom ones from many Add-Ons.
