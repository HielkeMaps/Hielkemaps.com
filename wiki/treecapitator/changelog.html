Added
  • Trees now fall over with a new toppling animation when cut down
  • "Fall Straight Down" animation: the tree sinks straight into the ground
  • "Disintegrate" animation: the tree dissolves block by block
  • Falling sounds: creaking, impact and rustling leaves
  • Big trees now shake the camera when they land (can be turned off)
  • Trees topple in the direction they're cut, or towards their heaviest side if one side is heavy enough
  • Setting for where tree items drop (at the stump or where they land)
  • Setting to limit the max tree size that animates
  • Axe Enchantments setting: Silk Touch and Fortune now apply to the whole tree
  • Auto Replant: a sapling is automatically planted at the stump, taken from the tree's drops or your inventory
  • New outline options: a rainbow color, a pulse effect and a thickness slider
  • Toppling trees can now hurt players they fall on, with a damage slider (off by default)
  • Support for the Oh The Biomes You'll Go Add-On
  • Support for poplar trees
